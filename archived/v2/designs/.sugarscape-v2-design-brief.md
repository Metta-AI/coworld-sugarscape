## Masthead / framing

- Title: "Sugarscape v2 — Design"
- Eyebrow: Design Document · 2026-08-07 · Status: Design complete — pre-implementation
- Audience: James (author/reviewer) and implementing agents/colleagues.
- One-line story for the executive summary: v2 is our OWN Sugarscape — not a
  port of the DTL Python model (decision D1) — rebuilt so that player agency
  is designed into every mechanic as an explicit gate, runnable identically
  as WASM Arena components, a WebSocket container, and a browser replay
  resimulator over ONE all-integer deterministic core. All 30 open questions
  (A1–F6) were settled 2026-08-07; this document is the record.
- The founding move to convey early: the classic hard-codes agent BEHAVIOR
  (movement rules, MRS-crossing trade, auto-mating, retaliation look-ahead);
  v2 evicts behavior to policies (P2) and keeps only physics. Every classic
  behavioral rule became either a policy gate or a negotiation protocol.

## Rejected alternatives (per decision — the doc MUST show these)

- **A1 total_agents:** rejected fixed-64 (certification must simulate 64
  agents; no density variants) and agents_per_seat-only parametrization
  (James preferred the explicit total + divisor validation).
- **A2 newborn assignment:** rejected any single fixed rule → configurable
  {random_parent, initiating_parent, receiving_parent}; rejected
  sim-controlled children (reintroduces v1's two-tier NPC wart; violates
  P1/P2); rejected fertility-off-in-ranked (punts the question).
- **A3 dropped seats:** rejected immediate death (infra noise shocks the
  ecology discontinuously for everyone else); rejected sim-owned fallback
  behavior (v1's candidates[0] generalized — requires the sim to implement
  exactly the behavior P2 evicted). Freeze = physics continues, no special
  code path.
- **A4 budgets:** rejected game-enforced time budgets entirely —
  inexpressible under Arena (deterministic guests have no clock), would make
  the two runtimes play different rules. Time is runtime-owned.
- **B1 length:** rejected 500 (mating/lending dynamics too shallow) and 2000
  (v1's league value; doubles compute, squeezes the 3600 s Arena wall clock).
- **B2+B3 wellness:** rejected ALL end-snapshot variants — dead=0 (one death
  zeroes `min`; t=999 loss erases the episode), welfare-at-death (dying rich
  early scores like living rich long), exclude-dead (culling your weak
  raises the reduce — monstrous). Rejected integrate-÷-ticks-alive (short
  spectacular life outranks long good one). Integrated ÷ T dissolves every
  pathology.
- **B4 CD form:** exponents uncontested (metabolism shares, the classic
  trade-generating choice). Gross-vs-net deferred INTO E6 rather than decided
  early — and E6 later resolved it (gross).
- **B5 reduce:** rejected mean (structurally punishes mating — late-born
  agents dilute), max (15 of 16 agents irrelevant; culling-by-neglect), min
  (every newborn is your minimum), median/geomean (tail-blind / one
  near-zero agent collapses geomean). `sum` was ADDED to the table — the
  only reduce where survival, prosperity, and reproduction all point the
  same way; bounded by carrying capacity.
- **B6 ties:** James rejected both pure-draws and full deterministic chains;
  chose survivors-then-draw. Implementation note: survivor count encodes
  lexicographically below welfare precision in the score scalar (F6 owns
  the layout).
- **C1 maps:** rejected v1-style procedural peaks spec (radial falloff math
  becomes normative spec every runtime must reproduce bit-for-bit) and
  both-formats (two validation/determinism stories). A map is data, not a
  program.
- **C2 seasons:** rejected fractional winter rates (floats in the
  environment) and per-resource climates (no precedent, double config).
- **C3 pollution:** rejected observation-only (provably inert under P2 —
  smart policies ignore costless data; the classic's pollution only worked
  through the hard-coded movement rule). Shipped BOTH physical effects
  configurable rather than picking one story.
- **C4 disease:** rejected simple SIR (flatter dynamics, generic immunity)
  and deferral (leaves holes in config schema and F1).
- **C5 resources:** James rejected first-class single-resource mode; chose
  always-both (uniform schemas; sugar-only = zero spice capacity + zero
  spice metabolism; no trade validation needed since trade is
  policy-negotiated — a spiceless world has nothing to exchange).
- **D1 max age:** rejected ranked-immortal and no-max-age (well-played
  agents live all 1000 ticks; fertility becomes optional; undermines B5).
- **D2–D4 death:** rejected inherit-else-vanish and always-vanish
  (deflationary, corpses inert), always-drop (removes the dynasty
  investment mechanic). No-replacement confirmed from P3+P1.
- **D5 genetics:** rejected all-fresh draws (mate choice strategically
  empty — hollows E5) and a genetics knob (untested second path, no demand).
- **E1 activation:** the assistant recommended simultaneous+lottery
  (fairness, latency); **James chose sequential seeded order for movement**
  — classic semantics valued over the WS-shell latency cost (consciously
  accepted; Arena is primary). Harvest/tribe order-free; negotiation phases
  get their own structures (E4/E5). Record this as a genuine
  human-judgment override of the recommendation.
- **E2 combat:** rejected probabilistic-by-wealth-ratio (adds outcome RNG,
  weakens wealth-as-armor) and unrestricted attack (kamikaze griefing is
  free for doomed agents). The classic retaliation look-ahead is evicted as
  behavior, not dropped as an oversight.
- **E3 harvest:** rejected all-or-nothing (pollution restraint collapses to
  binary; C3's levers lose their play).
- **E4 trade:** rejected sim-matched order book (the matcher re-centralizes
  what P2 decentralized; bilateral strategy — partners, reputation, boycott
  — dissolves) and free-form byte channel (protocol surface explodes; still
  needs the binding machinery; buildable later as a variant).
- **E5 mating:** rejected dropping the opposite-sex requirement (makes sex
  vestigial) and willingness-flags/sim-pairing (partner CHOICE disappears —
  hollows D5's genetic strategy).
- **E6 lending:** rejected rollover-on-shortfall (zombie contracts,
  compounding bookkeeping) and net-of-principal welfare (belt-and-suspenders
  against an exploit that due≤T + auto-collect already neutralize; would put
  principal tracking inside the scoring function). Key insight to preserve:
  under integrated welfare, interest is the price of welfare-time; CD
  concavity gains from rich→poor transfers (incl. E4 gifts) are legitimate
  distribution management under sum, not exploits.
- **E7 tribes:** rejected classic cultural tags (emergent/passive — nearly
  invisible to a policy trying to PLAY it; no phase 5), v1 seat-tribes
  (degenerates to everyone-their-own-tribe at 64 single-agent seats; zero
  strategic content), and deferral. Chosen allegiance is the one explicitly
  novel mechanic — flag as the design's biggest untested bet.
- **E8 ordering:** rejected metabolism-before-actions (dies before acting;
  breaks classic collect-then-metabolize grace) and loans-in-phase-0
  (tighter credit, lender-unfriendly).
- **E9:** rejected penalizing illegal actions (no-op = inaction; A3's freeze
  depends on inaction being legal).
- **F1 observation:** the assistant recommended full transparency; **James
  chose minimal visibility** ({seat, holdings, tribe, sick} only; max_age
  private to all) — "poker, not chess." Consequence flagged and accepted:
  mating proposals are partially blind; voided proposals ARE information;
  the scratchpad is where courtship inference accumulates. Another genuine
  override — record it as such. Square-radius vision also rejected (breaks
  vision=movement identity).
- **F2 scratchpad:** rejected per-seat pad (loses per-agent replay
  legibility) and dual pads (machinery without demand). Corrected premise
  recorded: component RAM persists per-episode, so pad scope was never an
  enforceable isolation boundary — honest-rules clause makes intra-seat RAM
  coordination legal.
- **F3 encoding:** rejected CBOR/MessagePack (decode tax on every debug
  session and LLM policy) and Sprite-v1-lineage binary (built for 24 tps
  pixel streams; wrong profile). JSON family matches every turn-based
  coworld in the ecosystem incl. v1.
- **F4 runtime:** rejected WS-first build order (delays the dogfood, builds
  the bigger shell before the core is proven).
- **F5 replay:** rejected pad snapshots in the base format (bloat; policy
  memory public by default).
- **F6 determinism:** rejected vendored-pow floats (floats re-enter the
  accumulating integral; contraction discipline returns) and
  minimal-spec-defer (replay exactness unproven until the choice is made
  anyway).

## Figures (inline SVG, house palette)

1. **The canonical timestep** — the centerpiece figure. Vertical flow:
   Phase 0 world updates (growback+seasons+suppression / pollution
   diffusion+decay / disease) → gates 1–5 (move/combat [sequential],
   harvest [fan-out], trade [R cycles], mate [R cycles], tribe) → Phase 6
   upkeep (loans → metabolism → aging → deaths → estates → welfare
   accrual). Annotate which phases invoke policies and which are pure
   physics.
2. **One core, three shells** — deterministic core (all-integer state,
   fixed-point welfare, seeded RNG, JSON codec, tick hash) feeding Arena
   components (softmax:game/player), WS container (COGAME_* contract), and
   browser replay resimulator; replay = config+seed+decision log flowing
   from any shell into the viewer.
3. **The scoring pipeline** — per-tick fixed-point CD welfare → per-agent
   integral (÷T; death truncates) → per-seat `sum` reduce → score scalar
   with survivor bits below welfare precision.
4. **E4 negotiation cycle** (small) — propose (one targeted offer) →
   respond (accept/decline) → seeded execution with voiding → next cycle or
   early end on zero offers; label "reused by mating (E5) and lending (E6)".

## Open questions & risks section (design is complete; risks remain)

- E7 chosen-allegiance tribes: the one mechanic with no literature; dynamics
  unobserved until playtesting. Fallback: feature-flag off.
- F1 minimal visibility: blind mating proposals — playtest; adding
  fertile_now visible is a one-line revision if courtship proves too random.
- 64 seats = 65 Arena component instances vs the 17 the arena-runner
  experiment was sized for — scaling question to raise with the Arena work
  (memory caps are host constants).
- Arena is experiment-scoped and its metta stack was unmerged as of
  2026-08-06 (jb/arena-task-* branches); the WS shell remains mandatory for
  league hosting.
- Fixed-point ln/exp (32.32) implementation must be written and
  parity-tested once, then frozen (pinned coefficients).
- trade_rounds/mating_rounds defaults (2–3) are placeholders pending
  playtesting.
- balance unknowns: combat α, pollution coefficients, K tribes default 2.
